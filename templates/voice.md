# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in the
pack reads it. Ten minutes here beats any prompt you'll ever write, because on
YouTube you have to say the words out loud, and a script in the wrong voice is
impossible to read on camera.

## Who I'm talking to

One person. Describe them properly - not "creators", but "someone under a
thousand subs who's made nine videos and can't work out why none of them get
past 400 views".

## How I really talk

Paste the transcript of your best-performing video here, or three of them. How
you actually sound, not how you wish you sounded.

## Words I never use

The ones that aren't yours. Be specific: "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in",
"let's get into it".

## Words I do use

Your habits. The phrases that are yours. Keep them.

## What I won't claim

Numbers I can't back up, results that aren't mine, tools I haven't used. Write
them down so the skills refuse them for you, instead of you catching them in
the edit.

## My format

Video length, how a video opens, whether there's an intro, whether you swear,
whether you're on camera, what the setup looks like.
